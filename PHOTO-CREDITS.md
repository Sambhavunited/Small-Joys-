Most photos on the site are Small Joys' own. The photos below are free-to-use stock photos standing in
until Deepika has her own picture of the same bake. They load from Unsplash and Pexels, whose licences allow
free commercial use with no attribution required. We credit the photographers here anyway.

- Unsplash licence: https://unsplash.com/license

To swap one for a real photo, add the picture to `public/images/` and change the `image` (or gallery `src`) in
`src/data/menu.ts`. The custom cakes page picture is set in `src/app/custom-cakes/page.tsx`.
